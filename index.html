    Questions

    What happens to our data?

    Your roster stays on your phone. What goes to the server is encrypted or scrambled on your phone, so the server can't read names, posts or your roster. A few optional features store what you give them: a sign-in email or phone number, a team name you choose to list, and a leader's contact for the Verified badge. Read the full privacy policy.

    Can I rely on it for emergencies?

    Accounted For is a tool that helps you count people. It does not replace your emergency plan, your school or employer's procedures, or calling 911. Always follow your official procedures.

    Who can see my team?

    People you invite. You choose who joins.

    What if there's no signal?

    A check-in saves on the phone and sends when you're back in signal.

    Is there an Android version?

    Android is in testing on Google Play now. The iPhone app is live, and the web app works in a browser.

    How much does it cost?

    Free for teams up to 10 people. Pro is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, for up to 50 people. Team is $12.99 a month for up to 100 people, plus $2.99 a month for every 50 more. Org is $29.99 a month or $249 a year for unlimited people.

    How do I delete my data?

    In the app, use Security, then Erase all app data on this phone, or delete the app. For anything stored on the server, email us and we'll delete what we can find within 30 days.
