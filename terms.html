Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

By joining a feed, signing in, or posting, you agree not to post or send content that is offensive, harassing, threatening, hateful, sexually explicit, violent, defamatory, illegal, or that shares someone else's private information without permission. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.

How we enforce it. Every Feed post has a ⋯ menu to Report the post, Hide it, or Block the person who posted it. Reports go straight to the person running that team. They must review each report and, when it breaks these rules, remove the post and cut off the poster's access within 24 hours. Blocking hides that person's posts on your phone at once.

If the person running your team doesn't act, email Antoniotyler22@gmail.com with the team name and what happened. We will act within 24 hours, which can include disabling the feed.

Who runs a team

The person who creates a feed, board or login is responsible for who they invite and for moderating what is posted there. Only the team leader and the section leaders they appoint can post to a Feed. Members can confirm posts, report problems, send requests and report posts.

Your account

Logins are created by the person running your team. You can delete your account at any time in Tools → Data and settings → Security → Delete my account. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored and deleted.
