Quick-start guide

For leaders, section leaders and members. Five minutes to get running.

1. Leader: first 10 minutes

  1. Open the app, pick your version (Military, Police, Jobs, HR or Civilian), and add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen).
  2. DATA → Load roster: your MOL detail list (CSV) or a backup. Rename tabs to match your sections.
  3. DATA → Security: set a PIN. DATA → Backup and sync: turn it on and save the recovery code somewhere safe.
  4. RECALL → Load phone numbers from your recall roster (Excel, PDF, Word or contacts).
  5. TEXT: add who you send numbers to and your unit name. Tap Text it.

2. Bring your section leaders in

  1. Board → Live sections → turn on → Text HE link (one per section).
  2. Each section leader loads the same roster, opens their link and taps Link and send now.
  3. From then on their changes reach your board in about 15 seconds, and their appointments show on your calendar.

3. Bring everyone in

  1. Feed → Start a feed → Invite people: one text with a join link. Post the word; people tap Got it.
  2. DATA → My pages → Turn on for everyone, then text each person their private link. They see their own status, duty, due-outs and calendar.
  3. Everyone can use MY LOG to write daily accomplishments and export them for evals.

💬 Help & support
  • Check in: pick a status or Other and write it in. Anything but On Hand asks From, To and Location. The leader taps Confirm and the roster updates.
  • Updates: when the leader changes their status or answers a check-in, they get an alert and tap Confirm.
  • Only they can read their page. It's locked with the key in their private link.

New in 1.4

  • Check-ins (Tools): people check in from My page or the Feed; you Confirm and the roster updates. Optional auto-confirm.
  • Activity: every update has Confirm, plus Confirm all.
  • Dates and location: any status but On Hand asks From, To and Location. Sections show when they were last updated.
  • People with access (Tools): everyone who can open your team, on your roster or not.
  • Invite: send a link with a role, or a link to start their own team, to any number or email.
  • Help & support (Tools or My page): ask anything about the app, or email a person.
  • The tour (Tools → Take the tour) now walks through all of this, with an example My page.

4. Daily rhythm

  • Morning: post the Plan of the Day in Feed; check the board and ALERTS.
  • Formation: MUSTER → Show QR, people check themselves in.
  • During the day: section leaders update; you send numbers from TEXT.
  • Weekly: READY for due-outs, DUTY for the roster, TRENDS for the brief, ⬇ Excel for the admin shop.

New in 1.3

  • Green dots show what's new. Tap the item and its dot goes away.
  • Missed check-in alerts: on a recall, set a deadline. You get one alert with how many haven't checked in, even with the app closed.
  • Rally point: people can confirm they made it. Only their distance is sent, never where they are.
  • PDF headcount report (Tools → PDF report), import any roster from Excel or CSV, and calendar export for Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
  • Slack, Teams or Discord (Tools → Integrations): post the numbers to a channel.
  • Group logins: make logins for a rank, a section or a few people at once. People set their own password and can log out.
  • Clear things off: due-outs get Done, Got it or Delete; alerts get Back ✓, Departed or Hide; calendar items get Done, Hide or ✕.
  • Made a mistake? "Hidden and cleared" at the bottom of Alerts, Readiness and Calendar lets you Unhide, Undo or Delete. Took someone off the numbers by mistake? Data → Fix a wrong list → Undo.

New in 1.2

  • Move people between sections: on a person's card pick a new section, or People → Move people between sections to move many at once.
  • 6W in your format: BLUF, Who, What, When, Where, Why, WAWDAI, FPC Status, Domicile, EAS. Email it or text it. "Edit the format" adds, removes, renames or reorders lines.
  • Account for anything: the Custom version lets you name the people and statuses yourself (a 5K, a trip, a class, a shift). Events have quick picks (5K run, TAD, Training…) and your own types.
  • See a person's My page: on their card tap View their My page to see exactly what they see.
  • Messages: private chat with each person (Tools → Messages, or Message on their card). They answer from their My page.
  • Pages: your own page (like a small Instagram) and group pages where people post, ask and give advice. Invite only.
  • Ask (AI) is now an optional Pro feature. Turn it on in Data and settings → AI.

New in 1.1

  • 🚨 Emergency muster (Home or Recall): one tap starts a live check-in, posts an URGENT Feed alert with the check-in link, and alerts you about anyone who misses the deadline.
  • Missed check-in alerts: during any recall, pick a deadline. When it passes, the app buzzes and lists who hasn't checked in, with one-tap text and call.
  • Dashboard (Tools → Dashboard): headcount, recall, requests, reported posts and Feed read receipts on one screen. Open it on a computer or iPad for the wide view.
  • Works offline: Got its, problems, requests and reports wait for signal and send by themselves.
  • Report, hide or block: tap ⋯ on any Feed post. Reports go to the person running the team, who reviews them within 24 hours (Feed → Reported posts).
  • Delete my account: DATA → Security → Delete my account removes your sign-in and everything you created on the server, then erases the phone.

5. Server setup (leaders who run their own copy)

  1. Create a free Supabase project. Put its URL and publishable key in the app (ask your developer).
  2. In the app: DATA → System check → Run. For anything with ❌, tap Copy part 1, paste in Supabase → SQL Editor → Run, then the next part.
  3. For push alerts: Edge Functions → create notify, paste the code from System check, add the VAPID_PUBLIC and VAPID_PRIVATE secrets, and turn off Enforce JWT verification.
  4. Run the three Server protection parts once.
  5. Updating to 1.1: run 16_v11 from System check, and redeploy notify so reported posts buzz your phone.
  6. Updating to 1.2: run 17_social from System check (messages and pages).

Privacy in one line

Your roster stays on your phone. What goes to the server is encrypted or scrambled, so the server can't read names, posts or your data (the optional server hub is the one exception, and it's marked). Use it on your own phone, and don't enter SSNs, DoD ID numbers (EDIPI), or classified or CUI information. Full privacy policy · Support · Open the app
