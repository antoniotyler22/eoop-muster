Privacy Policy

MusterBoard (shown in the app as EOP Numbers, Shift Numbers, Crew Numbers, Staff Numbers or Team Numbers, depending on the version you pick).
Effective October 22, 2026.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't run servers that receive your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't use analytics, ads or trackers. We can't see your roster.

What the app stores

Only what you type in or load from your own files: names, ranks or job titles, sections or teams, status and duty status, dates, locations or notes you add, phone numbers for a recall or call tree, equipment or asset assignments, incident (6W/IR) drafts, event attendance and app settings.

When you load a file, the app keeps only the columns it needs. ID numbers (EDIPI, Social Security), sex, emergency contact and next-of-kin columns are skipped and never stored.

Where it's stored

In your phone's local app storage for this app only. It is not uploaded anywhere. If you turn on PIN lock (DATA → Security), the stored data is encrypted on your phone with AES-256 using a key made from your PIN. There is no PIN recovery, so we can't unlock it either.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it: tapping Text it, Share, Copy, Call, or exporting a backup file. That data then goes through the app you pick (Messages, Signal, email and so on) under that app's privacy terms. Exported backup files are not encrypted, so handle them like any personnel document.

Reports can carry a short code line so a higher-level leader's app can add up the numbers (Rollup). That line holds counts only, no names. A recall check-in link holds your unit name, the recall time and, if you enter it, your cell number so replies come to you. Those details sit after the # in the link, which browsers never send to the website host. The person tapping the link chooses whether to send their reply.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-up, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and doesn't sell or share data.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when the app's files are downloaded; see GitHub's privacy statement. Those files contain no roster data. Fonts and file readers are served from the same site, not from third parties.

Deleting your data

Go to DATA → Security → Erase all app data on this phone, or delete the app. Either removes everything. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing teams. It isn't directed to children under 13.

Not a government system

MusterBoard is an independent app. It is not an official system of, and is not endorsed by, the Department of Defense, the U.S. Marine Corps, any military service, police agency or government. Follow your organization's rules on what personal information may be kept on personal devices.
