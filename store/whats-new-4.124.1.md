Accounted For 4.124.1
- Fixed: a status you pick by hand stays put. Before, some people (like a Sgt who had checked in earlier from another phone) could flip back to On Hand a few seconds after you changed them, because an older update from another phone or a section leader looked newer than your change. Now every status you pick is stamped with the time you picked it, and older updates can't overwrite it.
- People whose status was set by hand before this update may need to be set once more.
