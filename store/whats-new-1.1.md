# Accounted For 1.1 — What's New (paste into App Store Connect)

- 🚨 Emergency muster: one tap starts a live headcount and sends everyone the check-in link.
- Missed check-in alerts: set a deadline and get alerted about anyone who hasn't checked in, with one-tap text and call.
- Dashboard: headcount, recall, requests and read receipts on one screen. Great on iPad and desktop.
- Works offline: confirmations and requests send by themselves when you're back in signal.
- Report, hide or block any Feed post. Leaders review reports within 24 hours.
- Delete my account: remove your sign-in and your data from Settings → Security.
