## Full description

Know who's here, who's not, and who got the word, from your phone.

Accounted For (shown as EOP Numbers, Shift Numbers, Crew Numbers, Staff Numbers or Team Numbers) is built by a leader who ran 103 people and needed the numbers at his fingertips.

ACCOUNTABILITY
• Live board: on hand, present and strength by section, updated with a tap
• Section leaders update your board from their own phones, live
• Text the numbers up the chain in one tap; roll platoons up to company and beyond
• Formation muster with a QR code people scan to check themselves in

RECALL AND THE WORD
• Recall drills: everyone checks in Good, Delayed or Need help, live
• FEED: pass the word inside the app; see who confirmed and who didn't
• Plan of the Day, pinned posts, urgent posts and reminders
• Requests: leave, liberty and appointments, approved with one tap

READINESS AND SCHEDULE
• Due-outs: counselings, PFT/CFT, quals, PHA, licenses, with red/yellow alerts
• Calendar: appointments, leave, TAD, duty and due-outs for everyone
• Fair duty roster that skips people on leave or TAD
• Each person gets My page: their status, duty, due-outs and calendar

FOR EVERYONE
• My log: write down your accomplishments daily; export bullets for evals and awards
• Trends, Excel export, incident reports (6W), PT and event attendance

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your roster stays on your phone, with optional PIN lock and encryption
• Anything shared is encrypted or scrambled first; the server can't read names or posts
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• Works offline

Independent app. Not an official system of, or endorsed by, the DoD, any military service, police agency or government.
