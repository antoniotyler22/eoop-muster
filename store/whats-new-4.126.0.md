Accounted For 4.126.0
- Links open the app: invite, check-in and team links on accountedforteam.com open straight in the iPhone app.
- Reconnect to your roster: if your link stops working (your leader cut off old links or sent a new one), every screen says so and shows how to reconnect: paste the new link, text your leader for one, or check again. Nothing on your phone is deleted.
- One invite link: Give someone access is now a single invite link. Pick what they can do (view only, can edit, same as me), then share it, copy it or show a QR code. After "Cut off everyone" the new link is ready right away.
- Your name and role on everything: once you pick who you are, your posts, messages, replies and status changes all go out under your name, with your role ("Set by SGT Smith · Can edit").
- Choose your alerts: Settings → Alerts has a switch for each kind of alert. Section updates and team-change alerts start off and arrive silently; urgent alerts (SOS, need help, urgent posts, missed check-ins) always come through.
- The Dashboard shows the same numbers as Home: On hand of the roster, Off Hand counted in the breakdown, and recall check-ins counted.
- If saving or sharing a file fails, the app now says what went wrong, with Try again (and Copy instead for text files).
