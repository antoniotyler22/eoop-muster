What's new in 1.8

- Reply to check in: people texted the check-in (Pro) can just reply 1, 2 or 3. No app, no data, no link. Their reply counts them on your screen.
- Visitors and contractors: sign in visitors, contractors, vendors and deliveries. Everyone on site counts in the emergency check-in and the headcount.
- Drill history: every check-in you close out is logged with the time to get everyone, who was missing and who needed help. Export it for your safety records.
- Elapsed-time alarms: your phone buzzes every 2, 5 or 10 minutes with how many are still missing.
- School template: grades as groups, roll call, drills.
- Family "safe" texts: once a child is accounted for, tell their family with one tap, with the time and pickup spot.
- Faster roster import: paste rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets, with a blank template. Parent phone columns are read in, and missing numbers are filled in.
