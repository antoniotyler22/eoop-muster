**Accounted For 4.128 is out**

**Joining is easier**
- **Join code.** Every team has a 6-character code (no 0, O, 1 or I). Type it in Join a team, pick your name, and you're in. Owners can see it, make a new one, or turn it off in Settings → Tools → Join code.
- **Ask to join.** Find your team by name and send a request. Owners approve or decline in one tap, and you see the answer as soon as you open the app.
- **Sign in with Apple or Google.** If your email is on the roster, signing in puts you in your spot. If it isn't, you go straight to Ask to join.
- **Links open the app.** Invite, check-in and team links on accountedforteam.com open right in the iPhone app.
- **Reconnect to your roster.** If your link stops working, the app tells you on every screen and walks you back in. Nothing on your phone is deleted.

**Talking**
- **Message someone.** Pick a person and talk 1:1, with read receipts. Only the two of you can read it: not your leader, not the server.
- **Official channel and Voice** are now cards on My page.
- **Team chat is retired.** Use Feed for the team. Old chat messages stay under Feed, read-only.

**Your team**
- **One People screen.** Filter by section, select people and tap Move to… Owners see each section's leader and who's missing, and can assign a leader.
- **Calendar by section.** Everyone events reach every section. A section's events only reach that section.
- **Email on the roster.** Import it, edit it, export it.
- **Every team has a name.** It shows on every join screen.
- **Settings → Tools.** Turn any tool on or off for your phone. Its data stays saved.
- **Your name and role** show on your posts and status changes once you pick who you are.
- **Choose your alerts.** Section updates and team changes now start off and arrive silently. Urgent alerts always come through.

Update from the App Store, or open the web app at accountedforteam.com.
