**Accounted For 4.126 is out**

- **Links open the app.** Invite, check-in and team links now use accountedforteam.com and open straight in the iPhone app.
- **Reconnect to your roster.** If your link stops working, the app tells you on every screen and walks you back in: paste the new link, text your leader for one, or check again. Nothing on your phone is deleted.
- **One invite link.** Leaders: People → Live → Invite link. Pick view only, can edit or same as me, then share it, copy it or show a QR code. After "Cut off everyone" the new link is ready right away.
- **Your name and role on everything.** Once you pick who you are, your posts, messages and status changes show your name and role, like "Set by SGT Smith · Can edit".
- **Choose your alerts.** Settings → Choose alerts has a switch for each kind. Section updates and team changes now start off and arrive silently. SOS, need help, urgent posts and missed check-ins always come through.
- **Dashboard matches Home.** Same On hand count, Off Hand included, and recall check-ins counted.
- **Export errors are explained.** If saving or sharing a file fails, you'll see why, with Try again.

Update from the App Store, or reopen the web app at accountedforteam.com.
