# Accounted For 1.3 — What's New

- Missed check-in alerts: set a deadline on a recall and get one alert with how many haven't checked in, even with the app closed.
- PDF headcount report: totals, a table by section, who's out and where, and the recall, ready to share or print.
- Rally point: people can confirm they're at the rally point when they check in. Only their distance is sent.
- Import any roster: Excel or CSV with Name (or First and Last), Title, Team, Phone. New teams become sections.
- Slack, Teams and Discord: post the numbers to a channel in one tap, or automatically when you send them.
- Calendar export for Google, Outlook and Apple Calendar.
- Group logins: people can set their own password and stay signed in.
- Pro is now checked with Apple on the server, so Ask and the server hub work for your whole team.
- Crash reports (no personal data; can be turned off) so problems get fixed faster.
