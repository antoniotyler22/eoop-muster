Accounted For 4.128.0
- Join a team without a link: type the team's 6-character join code, find your team by name and ask to join, or sign in with Apple or Google (your leader's roster email finds your spot).
- Owners: approve or decline requests to join in one tap; see, renew or turn off your join code in Settings → Tools → Join code.
- Message someone: pick a person and talk 1:1. Only the two of you can read it, not your leader and not the server.
- Official channel and Voice are cards on My page. Team chat is retired; its old messages stay under Feed, read-only.
- Calendar: Everyone events show for every section; a section's events show only to that section.
- One People screen: filter by section, select people and Move to…; owners see each section's leader and who's missing.
- Email on the roster: import, edit and export it.
- Every team has a name; Settings → Tools turns each tool on or off for your phone.
