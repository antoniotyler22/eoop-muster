# What's new: Team (1.4)

- **Check in from your phone.** On My page (or the Feed), tap Check in, pick your status or Other and write it in. Anything but On Hand asks From, To and Location. Your leader confirms it and your page shows Confirmed.
- **Confirm check-ins.** Leaders get an alert, then Confirm puts it on the roster, or Not right sends a note back. Optional auto-confirm for people on your roster.
- **Confirm everything in Activity.** Every update on your team has a Confirm button, plus Confirm all.
- **Updates on My page.** When your leader changes your status or answers a check-in, you get an alert with Confirm.
- **Dates and location.** Setting anyone to Leave, TAD, an appointment or any status but On Hand asks From, To and Location, and every section shows when it was last updated.
- **People with access.** One list of everyone who can open your team, on your roster or not: My pages, logins, invites, check-ins and open links.
- **Start your own team** from My page.
- **Help & support.** Ask any question about the app and get an answer right away, or email a person.
