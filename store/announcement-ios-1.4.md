**Accounted For for iPhone 1.4 is out**

The iPhone app now has everything the web app has, through 4.120:
- Arrive check-in with a map of your muster zone. Leaders choose: ask people to confirm, or count on arrival.
- Undo if you're not there yet. You go back to Off Hand and aren't recounted that day.
- The app watches for you arriving while it's open.
- Your location stays on your phone. Leaders see counted, the time, and Auto.
