Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.

I'm getting a new phone. DATA → Export backup (.csv), move the file to the new phone, then DATA → Load roster and pick the backup.

I forgot my PIN. There is no recovery, because your data is encrypted with it. On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? twice to erase and start over, then load your last backup.

How do I switch between Military, Police, Jobs, HR and Civilian? Tap the app title at the top left.

Can several people update one board? Yes. Turn on Live sections from the board and section leaders update their own sections from their phones.

What is Pro? An optional subscription in the iPhone app for the person running the team: company roll-up, member logins, recall drills, QR muster, Excel export, trends and 6W reports. Members and section leaders never need it. New subscribers get 7 days free.

How do I cancel Pro? Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Accounted For Pro, or Tools → Pro and subscription → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I paid for Pro but it shows locked. Tools → See Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Text it doesn't open my messages. Use Copy text or Share instead and paste into your messaging app.

Not a government system

Accounted For is independent and is not endorsed by the Department of Defense, any military service, police agency or government. Follow your organization's rules for personal information on personal devices.
