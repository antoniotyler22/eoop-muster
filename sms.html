Text Messages (SMS)

Accounted For. Effective November 26, 2026.

What these texts are

Accounted For is a team check-in and headcount app. Team leaders (for example a teacher, coach, supervisor or unit leader) can have the app text the people on their own team roster. These are operational texts only, never advertising or marketing. We do not sell or share phone numbers.

The texts are sent from our toll-free number, +1 (888) 675-7006, in two situations:

  • A check-in the leader started. Example: "Alpha Team recall: tap to check in. [link] Or reply 1 = OK, 2 = delayed, 3 = need help." The person can tap the link or reply 1, 2 or 3 to be counted.
  • A "safe" notice (school version). A one-time message to a parent or guardian that their student is accounted for, and where pickup is.

How people agree (opt-in)

Texts go only to people a team leader has added to that leader's own roster in the app, and only when the leader starts a check-in. Before adding a phone number, the leader confirms that the person agreed to receive these texts from their team (for example by joining the team, filling in a sign-up or contact form, or telling the leader their number). The app shows this reminder next to the button that sends texts.

No one is texted for any other reason, and numbers are not collected from the public.

Frequency, cost and opting out

  • Frequency: only when a leader starts a check-in. Message frequency varies by team and is low, with a daily per-team limit.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply. Accounted For does not charge you to receive a text.
  • Stop: reply STOP to any text and you will receive no more from this number. You can also ask your team leader to remove your number.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email administration@accountedforteam.com or call 1-888-506-0639.

Your privacy

Phone numbers are used only to deliver the text and to match your reply to your check-in. They are passed to our text provider (Twilio) for delivery and are not kept for texting. Mobile numbers and opt-in information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
