Text Messaging Terms

Accounted For, operated by The Antwane Group INC. Effective November 26, 2026.

What these texts are

Accounted For is a team check-in app. Team leaders can send a check-in text to the members of their own team. The texts are account and safety notifications only: a check-in link, a reply prompt, and (school versions) a "safe" notice to a parent or guardian. We do not send marketing or promotional texts and we do not sell or share phone numbers for marketing.

Example check-in text: "Alpha Co recall: tap to check in. https://accountedforteam.com/app.html#ci=... Or reply 1 = OK, 2 = delayed, 3 = need help."

Example family notice: "Troop 12: Sam is accounted for and safe as of 14:30. Pickup: front gate. More updates will come by text."

How you opt in

You receive these texts only when a team leader or organizer has added your phone number to their team in Accounted For,. Our Terms of Use require every leader who uses the server text feature to add only numbers of people who agreed to receive team check-in texts (for example, a member who joined the team, or a parent or guardian who gave the number to the school, club or organizer for safety notices), and to remove a number when asked. Consent is not a condition of buying anything.

Frequency, cost and opting out

Message frequency varies with your team's activity, usually a few texts per check-in or drill. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts from this number. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at administration@accountedforteam.com or 1-888-506-0639. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

Phone numbers are passed to our messaging provider (Twilio) only to deliver the text. Replies are matched to the check-in with a one-way key and the number itself is not stored. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
